About

Precision & Presence

I build systems the way I build interfaces — precisely, with nothing left to chance.

I'm the founder of Hacktura R&D, a Kerala-based technology studio working across cybersecurity, embedded hardware, and software engineering. My work spans firmware for GPS-tracking hardware deployed with law enforcement, production Flutter applications, and hands-on security research — Active Directory environments, network defense, and offensive tooling. Alongside the engineering, I run Hacktura Hackers Academy, teaching the next group of practitioners the discipline behind the craft.

Philosophy

A disciplined approach to craft

Precision over polish. Discipline over shortcuts. Curiosity that never stops at "it works" — and execution that ships it anyway.

Every system I build — whether it's a firmware ring buffer or a login flow — is designed to fail safely, explain itself clearly, and hold up under scrutiny. That standard doesn't change based on who's watching.
